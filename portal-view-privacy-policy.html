Portal View Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Portal View is a Windows application developed by Craig Durrheim.

Information We Collect

Portal View does not require users to create an account and does not collect names, email addresses, passwords, payment information, or other personally identifiable information through the application.

Portal View does not intentionally collect or transmit personal information from users.

Images and Content

Portal View is designed to display visual content on a user's computer and connected secondary display. Content displayed through the application remains under the user's control.

Portal View does not upload users' images or other displayed content to a remote server.

Internet Access

The application may request internet access as part of its Windows application package. Portal View does not use this capability to collect or sell users' personal information.

Third-Party Services

Portal View does not intentionally share users' personal information with third parties.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to Portal View or its privacy practices. Any updated policy will be published on this page.

Contact

For questions regarding Portal View or this privacy policy, please use the contact information available on the Durrheim Luxury Lion Factory website.
