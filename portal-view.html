LEOPARD CHANGING SPOTS PRESENTS

Portal
View.

Experience video differently.

Portal View is a Windows application designed to enhance visual depth, detail and vibrant imagery when watching video on a secondary display.

Get Portal View ↗ Available on Windows

A secondary display is required.

01 THE IDEA

Don't just watch.
Experience the picture.

Portal View explores how software can change the way digital visuals are experienced. Its visual processing is designed to emphasise depth, bring out detail and create a vivid presentation of video content on a separate screen.

It is the first commercial software product from Leopard Changing Spots, built around experimentation, visual computing and the pursuit of a different viewing experience.

02 KEY FEATURES

Built around the visual experience.

Discover the visual qualities Portal View is designed to enhance.

Visual depth

Processing designed to give video scenes a stronger sense of depth and dimension.

Enhanced detail

Image processing designed to emphasise visible detail and definition.

Vibrant imagery

A vivid visual presentation that brings colour and contrast to your viewing experience.

Second-screen viewing

Designed for a Windows PC connected to a second monitor or television.

03 GETTING STARTED

Two screens.
A different perspective.

Portal View requires a secondary display. Connect a monitor or television to your Windows PC and configure Windows to extend your desktop.
